International Conference on Emerging Trends and Technologies in Applications of Computer Science

Invitation to Submit

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering, BIT Mesra, invites researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and students to submit original, unpublished research papers for presentation and publication at ICCETACS 2026.

The conference provides a premier interdisciplinary platform for sharing innovative ideas, emerging trends, and research findings in computer science and its real-world applications.

Submission Guidelines

Paper Format

Papers must be prepared in IEEE double-column format (A4 size) with a maximum of 6 pages including figures, tables, and references.

Language

All papers must be written in English with clear, concise, and grammatically correct language.

Originality

Papers must be original, unpublished, and not under consideration for publication elsewhere. Plagiarism will lead to immediate rejection.

Authorship

All authors must be listed with their full names, affiliations, and email addresses. Maximum 6 authors per paper.

Submission Portal

Submit papers through the official CMT (Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit) platform. Link will be provided soon.

File Format

Submit papers in PDF format only. Ensure all fonts are embedded and the file is print-ready.

Peer Review Process

1

Initial Screening

All submissions undergo an initial plagiarism check and format verification.

2

Double-Anonymous Review

Each paper is reviewed by at least 2-3 independent experts in the field.

3

Evaluation Criteria

Papers are evaluated based on originality, technical quality, significance, and clarity.

4

Decision

Authors receive one of three decisions: Accept, Accept with Revisions, or Reject.

5

Camera Ready

Accepted papers require final camera-ready submission with incorporated reviewer feedback.

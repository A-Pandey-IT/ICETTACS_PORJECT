Peer Review Process

Ensuring Academic Integrity and Quality Standards

International Conference on Emerging Trends and Technologies in Applications of Computer Science

Commitment to Quality

ICCETACS 2026 is committed to maintaining the highest standards of academic integrity and publication ethics. All submitted papers undergo a rigorous double-anonymous peer review process to ensure fairness, quality, and credibility.

Our review process is designed to provide constructive feedback to authors while ensuring that only high-quality, original research is presented at the conference and considered for publication.

Review Process Overview

01

Initial Submission & Screening

Authors submit their manuscripts through the CMT platform. The editorial team performs an initial check for:

  • Adherence to formatting guidelines (IEEE double-column format)
  • Plagiarism detection using iThenticate (similarity < 15%)
  • Scope alignment with conference themes
  • Basic language and technical quality
Duration: 3-5 days
02

Reviewer Assignment

Papers that pass initial screening are assigned to 2-3 independent reviewers based on:

  • Area of expertise matching paper topics
  • Conflict of interest avoidance
  • Reviewer availability and workload
Duration: 2-3 days
03

Double-Anonymous Review

Reviewers evaluate manuscripts based on the following criteria:

  • Originality: Novelty and contribution to the field
  • Technical Quality: Sound methodology and results
  • Significance: Impact and relevance to conference themes
  • Clarity: Organization, language, and presentation
  • References: Adequacy and relevance of literature review
Duration: 3-4 weeks
04

Review Compilation & Decision

Based on reviewer recommendations, the Technical Program Committee makes one of the following decisions:

  • Accept: Paper accepted for presentation and publication
  • Accept with Revisions (Minor/Major): Requires corrections before final acceptance
  • Reject: Paper not suitable for the conference
Duration: 5-7 days
05

Notification & Revision

Authors receive detailed review reports with constructive feedback. For accepted papers with revisions:

  • Authors must address all reviewer comments
  • Submit revised manuscript within specified deadline
  • Provide a point-by-point response to reviewers
Revision deadline: 10-15 days
06

Final Decision & Camera Ready

After satisfactory revisions, final acceptance is confirmed. Authors must:

  • Submit camera-ready manuscript in required format
  • Complete copyright transfer (if applicable)
  • Register for the conference
Final submission deadline: 10th June 2026

Ethical Standards

Double-Anonymity

Author identities are hidden from reviewers, and reviewer identities are hidden from authors to ensure unbiased evaluation.

Originality & Plagiarism

All submissions are checked for plagiarism. Papers with excessive similarity (>15%) or self-plagiarism are rejected.

Conflict of Interest

Reviewers must declare any potential conflicts of interest and recuse themselves from affected papers.

Timely Review

Reviewers commit to providing timely, constructive feedback within the designated timeframe.

Confidentiality

All submitted manuscripts are treated as confidential documents throughout the review process.

AI-Generated Content

Papers must be author-generated. AI tools cannot be listed as authors, and their use must be disclosed.

Guidelines for Reviewers

We appreciate our reviewers' valuable contributions to maintaining conference quality

Constructive Feedback

Provide specific, actionable comments that help authors improve their work. Critique the paper, not the authors.

Evaluation Criteria

Assess papers based on originality, technical quality, significance, clarity, and relevance to conference themes.

Timely Completion

Complete reviews within the allocated timeframe (typically 3-4 weeks). Request extension if needed.

Reporting Concerns

Report any suspected plagiarism, duplicate submission, or ethical violations to the Technical Program Committee.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the review process take?

The complete review process typically takes 6-8 weeks from submission to final decision, depending on reviewer availability and revision requirements.

Can I request specific reviewers?

Authors may suggest potential reviewers during submission. However, final reviewer assignment is at the discretion of the Technical Program Committee.

Is there a re-review process for revisions?

Yes, revised papers often undergo a second round of review to ensure all concerns have been adequately addressed.

What is the acceptance rate?

The acceptance rate varies each year based on submission quality. Previous ICCETACS conferences maintained an acceptance rate of 30-35%.

Can I appeal a rejection decision?

Rejection decisions are final. However, if you believe there has been a procedural error, you may appeal with valid justification.

Do reviewers receive recognition?

Yes, all reviewers receive a certificate of appreciation. Exceptional reviewers are recognized during the conference.

About The Institute & Department

Birla Institute of Technology (BIT), Mesra

Birla Institute of Technology (BIT), Mesra, established in 1955, is a premier technical institute in India known for its academic excellence, innovation, and contributions to engineering and research.

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) stands out as a hub of advanced learning, fostering both theoretical foundations and practical expertise in computing.

The department offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs in areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, data science, cybersecurity, and high-performance computing.

With a dynamic research environment supported by modern labs and global collaborations, the department nurtures innovation, critical thinking, and problem-solving skills.
